2. **You’ve described getting 82 comments on an early Microsoft code review. What were those engineers seeing that you weren’t?**

   **Context:** He recalls a roughly 50-line change receiving extensive criticism. Stay with what he actually changed in his work afterward. [His account, 15:29](https://github.com/ai-that-works/ai-that-works/blob/main/2025-12-30-founding-boundary/transcript.md).

3. **Your bootcamp made money, but you were unhappy running it. What was missing?**

   **Context:** LifePlusPlus taught programming to people entering tech. He says solo founding was miserable despite making money. Explore what he wanted his next company to give him beyond income. [His account, 18:18](https://github.com/ai-that-works/ai-that-works/blob/main/2025-12-30-founding-boundary/transcript.md).

4. **What’s a decision Aaron talked you out of that you’re now grateful for?**

   **Context:** Aaron is his co-founder. Their disagreements included returning to Gloo and continuing BAML. Let him pick a moment that shows how they make difficult decisions together. [Background](https://boundaryml.com/blog/5-years-12-pivots).

5. **You’ve said you held on to Gloo too long. Early BAML customers also pushed back. What was different that made BAML worth sticking with?**

   **Context:** Gloo was their voice-first workplace collaboration product. They abandoned it, but kept developing BAML despite early resistance. Focus on the evidence they had at the time. [Background](https://boundaryml.com/blog/5-years-12-pivots).

6. **You turned down a proposed $15,000-a-month search contract. What did you think accepting it would commit you to?**

   **Context:** Before BAML, they sold custom embeddings and questioned whether the approach could scale. This was a proposed contract, not existing revenue. Explore the direction that customer would have taken them. [Background](https://boundaryml.com/blog/5-years-12-pivots).

7. **When early customers wanted to stay with Python, how did you work out whether a new language was solving their problem or yours?**

   **Context:** BAML grew from frustration with existing AI libraries, including their own. Ask what became easier for an actual customer; this is about usefulness, while question 5 is about persistence. [Customer response](https://boundaryml.com/blog/5-years-12-pivots), [his design argument](https://boundaryml.com/blog/ai-agents-need-new-syntax).

8. **Sam wrote that he chose Boundary over OpenAI after another call with you. What could you honestly promise him at that stage?**

9. **What has building the company cost you that you didn’t properly account for at the start?**

   **Context:** He has described lost time with people he cares about as a major cost. Let him choose the example, then explore whether his priorities have changed. [His account, 1:04:41](https://github.com/ai-that-works/ai-that-works/blob/main/2025-12-30-founding-boundary/transcript.md).
