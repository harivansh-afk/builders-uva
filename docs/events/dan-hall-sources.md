# Dan Hall event sources

Checked September 16, 2026.

- [Legion Capital](https://www.legioncapitalllc.com/) lists Dan as a managing partner and describes his focus on people, ManTech career, Air Force service, and VMI degree. The supplied speaker bio confirms these career details. The site has an old copyright date; do not use its Kyrus CEO title as proof of his current title after the acquisition.
- [Legion's company history and portfolio](https://www.legioncapitalllc.com/storytime.html) describe Kyrus's incubation of Carbon Black and list Carbon Black, Red Canary, and Appsulate. The search index provided these pages; direct HTTPS retrieval failed certificate verification during this check.
- [SIXGEN acquisition announcement, December 2, 2024](https://washingtonharbour.com/kyrus-tech-and-sixgen-join-forces-to-create-preeminent-mid-tier-across-the-digital-battlespace/) confirms the acquisition of Kyrus and Dan's role as its CEO at the time. No sale price is given.
- [VMware acquisition announcement, October 8, 2019](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2019/10/08/1926966/0/en/VMware-Completes-Acquisition-of-Carbon-Black.html) gives Carbon Black's enterprise value as $2.1 billion. This is a company transaction value, not Dan's proceeds or proof that he personally led this sale.
- [Red Canary company page](https://redcanary.com/company/) lists Dan on its board. [Zscaler's August 1, 2025 announcement](https://ir.zscaler.com/node/15086) confirms the completed acquisition.
- [Zscaler annual report](https://ir.zscaler.com/static-files/047aee1c-2664-455b-b391-116c57efaf28) confirms the May 29, 2019 acquisition of Appsulate.
- The Kyrus logo is the official header image from [kyrus-tech.com](https://www.kyrus-tech.com/): [source image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c2a46b65cfd792a15793311/f00be27b-ca21-4d04-86b3-13fb60348a0f/2022+Title+wiki.png).
