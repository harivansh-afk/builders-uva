## Weekly rhythm

- Group check-in, once a week, 45 minutes. Each member gets five minutes: what shipped, what the numbers say, what is next, where they are stuck.
- One-on-one with their mentor, once a week, 30 minutes.
- Async update in the group chat by Sunday night: three lines, same questions.

## What members get

- A grant for their project. Non-dilutive; we take no equity.
- A weekly 1:1 with a mentor and the weekly group check-in.
- Two or three dinners at good restaurants in Charlottesville.
- Intros to people who can help, and a slot at the showcase in front of investors.
- A place in the founding cohort. These are likely the people who run the next one.

## What members owe

- Show up to every check-in, 1:1 and dinner. Miss two without a reason and you lose your spot.
- Send the Sunday update every week.
- Ship something real and present it on Dec 3.
- Sign the commitment at kickoff.
