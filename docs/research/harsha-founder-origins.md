# Harsha Gaddipati: formative years and the move into founding

Checked September 13, 2026. Focused additions to [the existing historical memo](./harsha-slashy-history.md), covering his college work, career choice and changing responsibilities. Public-source history only; no interview prompts.

## The college years included conventional engineering experience

In a retrospective published **May 7, 2025**, Harsha describes searching for internships as a freshman and getting his State Farm opportunity through a club meeting. He says his next search went better after emphasizing projects and how he presented his work, and that he then landed an Amazon internship. This establishes the sequence **State Farm, then Amazon**, but does not independently establish exact employment dates or teams. The account promotes a career Q&A, so its application/interview totals should be treated as self-reported; they are unnecessary to the basic historical account. [Harsha's internship-search post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/harsha-not-hiring-gaddipati-032bb820a_when-i-was-a-freshman-in-college-i-applied-activity-7325912178991992834-GvXG).

Alongside industry experience, he describes teaching-assistant work in physics, discrete math and Grand Challenges, and support from CREATE-X and Grand Challenges for his ventures. He completed the degree while already promoting Dash. [Harsha's graduation announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/harsha-gaddipati-032bb820a_georgiatech-graduation-activity-7324763127533707265-DJBp).

## Research was another substantial part of that period

Cambridge published **“Challenges in using satellite data for non-remote sensing specialists, an exploratory case study” on March 10, 2025** in *Data & Policy*. Harsha is the first and corresponding author. The contribution statement credits him with leading the writing and contributing to analysis, investigation, data curation, methodology, software, visualization and validation. The manuscript was received March 3, 2024 and accepted January 23, 2025, placing the work before graduation. [Cambridge publication record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/data-and-policy/article/challenges-in-using-satellite-data-for-nonremote-sensing-specialists-an-exploratory-case-study/D65CFBF5A4B76F1B625D225A6B896C3E), [published paper and contribution statement](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/D65CFBF5A4B76F1B625D225A6B896C3E/S2632324925000070a.pdf/challenges_in_using_satellite_data_for_nonremote_sensing_specialists_an_exploratory_case_study.pdf).

The paper examines the team's difficulty using satellite data to study megaregions despite data being openly available. Its subject is usability and access for non-specialists. This is evidence of interdisciplinary research experience; no source reviewed says the paper caused his subsequent startup choices. The journal is *Data & Policy*, not *Space Policy*. [Cambridge paper](https://doi.org/10.1017/dap.2025.7).

## He explicitly describes choosing the startup over an AWS offer

In an **August 19, 2025** post, Harsha says he rejected a full-time return offer following his AWS internship. His stated reason is wanting to build with greater speed and autonomy after finding meetings and approvals frustrating. He contrasts that experience with developing Slashy from Georgia Tech. This is his own retrospective explanation, not an independently verified description of the employer, and the post does not date when he declined the offer. It supports a conscious career choice without needing to invent external pressure, a personal crisis or a dropout story. [Harsha's explanation of declining AWS](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/harsha-gaddipati-032bb820a_i-turned-down-a-full-time-offer-from-amazon-activity-7363610920901595136-Pl98).

By **July 2, 2025**, his own YC announcement already names Dash and thanks Georgia Tech CREATE-X and Afore Capital as investors. This confirms that his postgraduation startup work had institutional support by then; the post does not establish investment amounts or acceptance date. [Harsha's YC announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/harsha-gaddipati-032bb820a_plot-twist-excited-to-announce-that-activity-7346157895027216384-Zs_e).
