### Organization description summary

Builders at UVA brings students together to build their own ideas, learn from founders, and help each other make progress. We host Thursday founder talks and Q&A sessions and welcome students from all majors and experience levels.

### Organization description

Builders@UVA is a student-led community that builds a culture of independent creation at UVA. We bring students together to work on their own ideas, learn from founders, and help each other go beyond their perceived limitations.

Our Thursday founder talks and Q&A sessions give students practical lessons about finding problems, building products, reaching customers, and learning from failure. We aim to pair these conversations with shared work sessions and peer feedback so members can turn ideas into working projects.

Students from all majors and experience levels are welcome. Prior technical skills, a startup, or a finished project are not required. Members can explore an idea, find collaborators, share progress, and learn by doing.

We value initiative, original thinking, constructive feedback, and repeated practice. Our goal is to make independent creation a normal part of student life.

## 5. New organization details

### Membership

Membership is open to all currently enrolled UVA students who support the organization’s purpose and register on its roster. No prior experience, completed project, competitive application, interview, or dues are required. The form at https://uva.builders/apply is an open registration and member-expertise survey, not a selection process. Under our constitution, UVA student members on the roster for at least 14 days may vote. The club follows UVA’s required nondiscrimination policy.

### Reason for CIO status

We seek CIO status to give this student-led community a stable structure, access to meeting spaces, and visibility through Hoos Involved. Recognition will help us maintain clear officer duties, fair elections, records, and continuity after current leaders graduate. We help students turn their own ideas into projects through founder conversations and peer support, with no required dues or prior experience. We do not plan to request SAF or Cav Funds this year.

### Target audience

Our primary audience is UVA students who want to create something of their own, including software, products, creative projects, or ventures. We welcome all majors, undergraduate and graduate students, beginners and experienced builders, and both technical and nontechnical participants. Interest and willingness to learn matter more than a resume, a business idea, or prior startup experience.

### Recruitment

Students can join through https://uva.builders/apply. The form surveys members’ experience; it does not select or reject applicants. We recruit at the start of the Fall and Spring semesters and welcome students throughout the year. Outreach includes public event notices, Hoos Involved, member referrals, and class or student-group announcements where permitted. No prior experience or interview is required.

### Retention

We will use a consistent Thursday event schedule, relevant founder Q&A sessions, and friendly peer support to make participation useful. Proposed work sessions and project demos will give members regular chances to share progress, get help, and find collaborators. We will welcome new participants, invite feedback after events, offer small volunteer roles, and publish short recaps for members who cannot attend. Participation will remain flexible around coursework, and progress will be encouraged without pressure or hazing.

### Officer elections

Under our constitution: Elect the President, Vice-President, and Treasurer once each year in March, with at least 14 days’ notice of nominations and the election. Complete the handover in April before new terms begin May 1. Hold a special election for a vacancy within 30 days when the academic calendar permits.

### Officer terms

One year, May 1 through April 30. A vacancy appointment serves the remainder of the term.

### Voting

Under our constitution: Each currently enrolled UVA student member listed on the roster at least 14 days before the election has one vote. A majority of eligible voting members constitutes quorum. Elections use a secret ballot, including an accessible remote option. A candidate must receive a majority of votes cast; if no candidate does, hold a runoff between the top two candidates.

### Officer removal

Under our constitution: A written request stating the grounds for removal may be submitted by at least three voting members. Grounds include serious misconduct, misuse of funds, or repeated failure to perform stated duties. Give the officer written notice at least seven days before a removal vote and an opportunity to respond. An uninvolved member chairs the process; the officer does not preside over or vote on their own removal. Removal requires a two-thirds vote of votes cast by eligible members at a meeting with quorum. Record the decision, protect private information, and fill any vacancy through a special election. Urgent safety concerns will be referred to the appropriate UVA office.

### Unique mission and programs

Builders at UVA focuses on independent creation: helping students make progress on ideas they choose themselves, whether or not those ideas become companies. Our current program is a Thursday founder talk and short Q&A series. We propose to connect that series to recurring peer work sessions and project demos, so members can apply what they hear, share what they tried, and learn from one another.

Our model has open membership, no required dues, no prior technical experience requirement, and no requirement to pitch a company or pursue investment. Its value is a consistent community around the practice of building and learning. We recognize that other UVA organizations support entrepreneurship and may offer similar events. We would coordinate calendars, share referrals, and seek joint events where useful, while keeping our own activities focused on members’ independent projects and progress.

### Similar organizations

1. Virginia Entrepreneurship Organization (VEO): Based on our organizers’ current knowledge, VEO is no longer operating. We address it because its Hoos Involved listing remains and describes a similar focus on side projects, startups, workshops, and founder talks. That listing also describes interviews and selective entry. Builders at UVA is actively holding weekly founder talks and offers open participation with no interview, prior technical experience, or company requirement. We plan peer work and feedback around members’ self-directed commercial or noncommercial projects. We do not claim that founder talks alone are unique or that UVA has formally deactivated VEO.

2. Virginia Venture Fund (VVF): VVF describes investment-focused analyst training, partner-firm experience, and an impact fund. Builders at UVA focuses on creating and testing members’ own projects rather than investment analysis. Our membership is open and does not require finance training or a selection process.

The Foundry is a relevant UVA resource for venture advising, events, and workspace. We refer members to useful campus resources and may seek collaboration; we do not claim a formal partnership. Sources: https://hoosinvolved.virginia.edu/organization/virginiaentrepreneurshiporganization ; https://www.virginiaventurefund.com/ ; https://innovates.virginia.edu/foundry/about-foundry
