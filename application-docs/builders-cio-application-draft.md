## 5. New organization details

### How does your constitution define membership?

Membership is open to all currently enrolled UVA students who support the organization's purpose and register on its roster. No prior experience, completed project, competitive application, interview, or dues are required. The form at https://uva.builders/apply is an open registration and member-expertise survey, not a selection process. Under the constitution draft, UVA student members on the roster for at least 14 days may vote. The club follows UVA's required nondiscrimination policy.

### Why seek CIO status?

We seek CIO status to give this student-led community a stable structure, access to meeting spaces, visibility through Hoos Involved, and eligibility to request Student Council funding for accessible programs. Recognition will help us maintain clear officer duties, fair elections, financial records, and continuity after current leaders graduate. Our purpose is to help students turn their own ideas into projects through founder conversations and peer support, with no required dues or prior experience.

### Target demographic

Our primary audience is UVA students who want to create something of their own, including software, products, creative projects, or ventures. We welcome all majors, undergraduate and graduate students, beginners and experienced builders, and both technical and nontechnical participants. Interest and willingness to learn matter more than a resume, a business idea, or prior startup experience.

### New member recruitment

Students can join through https://uva.builders/apply. The form surveys members' experience; it does not select or reject applicants. We recruit at the start of the Fall and Spring semesters and welcome students throughout the year. Outreach includes public event notices, Hoos Involved, member referrals, and class or student-group announcements where permitted. No prior experience or interview is required.

### Membership retention

We will use a consistent Thursday event schedule, relevant founder Q&A sessions, and friendly peer support to make participation useful. Proposed work sessions and project demos will give members regular chances to share progress, get help, and find collaborators. We will welcome new participants, invite feedback after events, offer small volunteer roles, and publish short recaps for members who cannot attend. Participation will remain flexible around coursework, and progress will be encouraged without pressure or hazing.

### Officer elections

Proposed rule: Elect the President, Vice-President, and Treasurer once each year in March, with at least 14 days’ notice of nominations and the election. Complete the handover in April before new terms begin May 1. Hold a special election for a vacancy within 30 days when the academic calendar permits.

### Officer terms

One year, May 1 through April 30. A vacancy appointment serves the remainder of the term.

### Who may vote?

Proposed rule: Each currently enrolled UVA student member listed on the roster at least 14 days before the election has one vote. A majority of eligible voting members constitutes quorum. Elections use a secret ballot, including an accessible remote option. A candidate must receive a majority of votes cast; if no candidate does, hold a runoff between the top two candidates.

### Officer removal

Proposed rule: A written request stating the grounds for removal may be submitted by at least three voting members. Grounds include serious misconduct, misuse of funds, or repeated failure to perform stated duties. Give the officer written notice at least seven days before a removal vote and an opportunity to respond. An uninvolved member chairs the process; the officer does not preside over or vote on their own removal. Removal requires a two-thirds vote of votes cast by eligible members at a meeting with quorum. Record the decision, protect private information, and fill any vacancy through a special election. Urgent safety concerns will be referred to the appropriate UVA office.

### How are the mission and programs unique?

Builders@UVA focuses on independent creation: helping students make progress on ideas they choose themselves, whether or not those ideas become companies. Our current program is a Thursday founder talk and short Q&A series. We propose to connect that series to recurring peer work sessions and project demos, so members can apply what they hear, share what they tried, and learn from one another.

Our proposed model has open membership, no required dues, no prior technical experience requirement, and no requirement to pitch a company or pursue investment. Its value is a consistent community around the practice of building and learning. We recognize that other UVA organizations support entrepreneurship and may offer similar events. We would coordinate calendars, share referrals, and seek joint events where useful, while keeping our own activities focused on members’ independent projects and progress.

### Two similar organizations and differences

1. **Virginia Entrepreneurship Organization (VEO):** VEO’s published description supports students working on side projects and startups through workshops, entrepreneur talks, and project questions. This is a close overlap. Builders@UVA’s proposed emphasis is a regular build-and-share practice around students’ self-directed projects, including projects with no commercial goal, supported by our Thursday founder Q&A series. We will not claim that founder talks alone are unique; the case for a separate organization is the sustained peer practice and community we plan to operate.
2. **Virginia Venture Fund (VVF):** VVF describes an investment-focused program with training in accounting, valuation, and investing, partner-firm projects, and a student-managed investment fund. Builders@UVA focuses on creating and testing members’ own projects rather than investment analysis. It has open membership and no finance training or selection process as a condition of joining.

The Foundry is also a relevant UVA resource for venture advising, workshops, and workspace. We would refer members to those services and explore collaboration; we do not claim a formal partnership.

The VEO comparison uses its October 2024 published description. Verify its current mission before submission. A proposed difference is not proof that Student Council’s non-duplication requirement is met.
